Your care journey

The right care at every stage

Start the day you find out, or join at any month. Tests and vaccines always follow your doctor's advice. We schedule them, remind you and take care of the rest.

Weeks 1–8

"I just found out"

We welcome you, check your health and set up your first appointments.

  • Home nurse visit: BP, weight, Hb, sugar
  • First appointment with a partner gynaecologist
  • Blood tests collected at home
  • Dating scan booking (6–9 weeks)
  • Nausea-friendly diet plan, veg/Jain options
  • Supplement & rest reminders
Weeks 9–13

End of trimester 1

Important screening scans, all scheduled for you.

  • NT scan & double marker (11–14 weeks)
  • Monthly nurse visit & vitals log
  • Vaccine reminders as per your doctor
  • Dietitian review
  • Gentle walking plan
  • Family session: how to support mum
Weeks 14–27

Trimester 2

The comfortable months: move, learn and get ready.

  • Anomaly scan (18–20 weeks)
  • Sugar test (OGTT) at home, 24–28 weeks
  • Prenatal yoga twice a week
  • Iron- & calcium-rich diet
  • Childbirth classes for couples
  • Add-on: garbh sanskar sessions
Weeks 28–31

Month 7

Closer care begins: nurse visits every two weeks.

  • Daily kick-count tracker with alerts
  • Breastfeeding class
  • Back & pelvic-pain physio
  • Hospital tour & birth plan
Weeks 32–35

Month 8

Get your home and family ready.

  • Meet your daai-maa / japa caregiver
  • Nursery & safe-sleep setup
  • Orientation session for elders
  • Insurance cashless help
Weeks 36–40

Pre-delivery

We're on standby, day and night.

  • Weekly nurse visits
  • 24×7 nurse helpline & SOS
  • Hospital-bag checklist
  • On-call cab / ambulance
Hospital

Delivery

You focus on your baby; we handle the rest.

  • Family updates, including relatives abroad
  • Home sanitised before you return
  • Caregiver arrives on discharge day
  • Nurse visit within 24 hours

Our daai-maa

The wisdom of dadi-nani, with the safety of a nurse

Every caregiver is interviewed, police-verified and trained in our academy before she enters your home. She learns traditional massage along with hygiene, safe oils and newborn warning signs.

How we select caregivers
Verified

ID, address and police clearance for every caregiver. Only about 1 in 6 applicants is selected.

Trained & certified

7–15 day academy, monthly refreshers, re-training if ratings drop.

Same caregiver

One primary caregiver for your family, with a backup you meet in advance.

Nurse-supervised

Every visit is logged. Warning signs go to our clinical lead within minutes.
