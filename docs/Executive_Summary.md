# Aanchal — Mother & Baby Care · Executive Summary

**What:** Aanchal is a home-care platform for mothers in the Chandigarh Tricity. It covers the whole journey from the first month of pregnancy until the baby is 18 months old.

**Why:**
- India has about 23 million births a year.
- Mothers usually leave hospital within 48 hours.
- The 40-day jaapa period is still handled by informal, unverified helpers who charge about ₹15k–35k a month.
- No Tricity provider joins pregnancy, delivery and baby care in one app. National players such as Apollo HomeCare do not list Chandigarh.

**Services (stage-wise):**
- **Month 1–2:** onboarding nurse visit, first gynae appointment, home lab tests, dating scan, diet plan.
- **Month 3:** NT scan and double marker scheduling, monthly nurse visits, walking plan.
- **Months 4–6:** anomaly scan, OGTT, prenatal yoga, childbirth classes, iron- and calcium-rich diet.
- **Months 7–9:** visits every two weeks, then weekly; kick-count tracking; breastfeeding class; caregiver matching; hospital bag; 24×7 helpline; cab or ambulance.
- **Post-delivery:**
  - Nurse visit within 24 hours of discharge.
  - Trained daai-maa baby massage and the baby's first bath.
  - Mother massage, japa shifts, lactation support and physiotherapy.
  - Postpartum depression (EPDS) screening.
- **Baby 0–18 months:** vaccines, growth tracking, weaning plans and paediatric access.
- **Families:** night japa and a "Dadi-on-call" mentor for nuclear families; elder orientation and shared family accounts for joint families.

Plans load **Care Credits** into a wallet, which can also be spent on add-ons. EMI and gifting are available.

**Model:**
- **Hybrid staffing:** daai-maa, japa caregivers and GNM/ANM nurses are in-house. Doctors, hospitals and labs are partners paid on commission.
- **Inspired by Pronto:** a three-sided platform (customer app, caregiver app, ops console) with hubs, strict screening and training, and auto-reassignment when a caregiver can't attend.

**Tech:** ASP.NET Core Web API and MVC admin panel, EF Core, SQL Server, and a React Native Android app.
