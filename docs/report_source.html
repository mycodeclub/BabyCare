At a glance

Executive summary

Indian families still rely on word-of-mouth for the most important 18 months of their lives. Pregnancy care is scattered across doctor visits, WhatsApp forwards and relatives' advice; after delivery, an unverified daai or japa maid is hired informally. Aanchal brings all of it into one trusted, app-led service.

births a year in India[4]

The opportunity

  • No Tricity provider covers pregnancy → delivery → 18 months end-to-end.
  • National home-health brands (e.g. Apollo HomeCare) do not list Chandigarh; local agencies publish no prices and have no app.
  • Traditional baby massage is in high demand but delivered by untrained, unverified individuals.
  • Pronto has proved that verified, trained, app-dispatched home workers scale fast (≈18k bookings/day within ~18 months)[2].

What we offer

  • Stage-wise care from month 1 of pregnancy: nurse visits, diet, scans & appointments, yoga, classes.
  • Post-delivery: daai-maa baby & mother massage, first bath, japa shifts, lactation, physio.
  • Baby 0–18 months: vaccines, growth, weaning, sleep, paediatric access.
  • Full plans (9/12/18 months) and small packs (40 days, 3 months, single visits).

Operating model

Hybrid. Core caregivers (daai-maa, japa, GNM/ANM nurses) are in-house, trained and verified. Doctors, hospitals, labs and physios are partners on commission. Nurse visits go to in-house staff first.

Technology

.NET MVC admin panel + ASP.NET Core Web API · EF Core · SQL Server · React Native Android app (customer now, caregiver later).

Roadmap

Plan & templates (now) → API + admin (10 wks) → customer app (10 wks) → 3-month pilot in 2 Tricity zones → caregiver app & Punjab expansion.

Brand name options

02 · Market gap

India has the tradition, but no industry

The 40-day jaapa (Ayurveda's sutika paricharya) is one of the world's oldest postpartum traditions. Yet there is no licensing category, no standard training and no insurance for it — while China, Taiwan and Korea have turned the same tradition into regulated industries.[4]

Where families struggle today

Qualitative severity ranking from desk research — to be validated with 50+ family interviews in Tricity (see Next steps).

Why now

  • Proven model: Pronto, Snabbit and Urban Company InstaHelp show that families will book verified home workers through an app; Pronto grew from 1 hub to ≈18k bookings a day.[2][3]
  • Nuclear families are growing; grandparents often live in another city or abroad.
  • Higher spend per child: families with one or two children invest in quality care.
  • Data-protection clarity: the DPDP Act 2023 sets rules for consent-based health data.
  • Hospitals want continuity: maternity hospitals lose contact after discharge — partners for referral.

The care gap we fill

PregnancyDoctor visits every 4 weeks; nothing in between at home.
→
Delivery1–3 days in hospital, then discharged.
→
Day 3 – 40Biggest need, least support. Informal maids, no checks.
→
Months 2–18Vaccines & milestones tracked on paper or not at all.
Aanchal's promise: one care manager, one app and one trained team for the whole journey, so no stage is left uncovered.

08 · Built for Indian families

Nuclear & joint families

A mother's care is a family affair. The app gives each family member a role, and services adapt to who is at home.

Nuclear family

Working couple, grandparents in another city or abroad.

  • Night-shift japa so parents can sleep and work
  • "Dadi-on-call": an experienced mentor for everyday doubts
  • Remote family dashboard: grandparents see visit logs, photos, milestones
  • Back-to-work plan, trusted nanny matching after month 3
  • Grocery & pharmacy reminders, appointment cab booking
  • Paternity-support tips and a dad checklist

Joint family

Elders at home with strong traditions and many hands to help.

  • Elder orientation session: keep valued traditions, avoid unsafe ones (e.g. kajal in eyes, honey for newborns)
  • Traditional daai-maa massage that elders recognise, with modern hygiene
  • Shared family account with roles: mother, husband, mother-in-law, caregiver
  • Hindi / Punjabi-speaking staff; festival-aware scheduling
  • Ceremony coordination: godh bharai, chhati, naming, mundan
  • Diet plans that fit the family kitchen

Referral & growth engine

Mothers trust other mothers. Referral is the primary acquisition channel, supported by partnerships with doctors and hospitals.

Customer referral flow

1 · ShareMother shares her code / link (WhatsApp, Instagram).
→
2 · JoinFriend signs up, gets ₹500 credits on first booking.
→
3 · BookFriend completes first paid service ≥ ₹999.
→
4 · RewardReferrer gets ₹500 credits; ₹2,000 if the friend buys a plan.

Customer → customer

Two-sided credits, family invites, milestone badges ("3 friends helped").

Doctor / hospital → customer

Partner-specific QR codes at OPD & discharge desk. Partner earns commission per plan; tracked in the admin.

Caregiver → caregiver

Staff referral bonus for recruiting trained daais and nurses after their 60-day retention.

Other channels

  • Free pregnancy-care camps in RWAs & sectors, with a nurse BP/Hb check
  • Instagram content in Hindi/Punjabi: "Dadi ke nuskhe vs doctor ki salah"
  • Corporate HR tie-ups: IT parks in Mohali, Panchkula IT Park, Chandigarh offices
  • Pharmacy & baby-store counters with QR
  • Google Business profile per hub; SEO for "baby massage Mohali" etc.

Referral controls (admin)

  • Campaign rules: reward amount, eligibility, expiry, caps
  • Fraud checks: same device / phone / address, self-referral
  • Partner commission ledger & monthly payout report
  • Coupon codes by zone and channel; conversion funnel
